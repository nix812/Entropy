# Tutorial: Basic Training, the Field Manual and first-time cards (builder "tutorial", for 5.8)

Nic: "Make a tutorial level for first time starters", then (his brother couldn't tell what to do, didn't get upgrading) "a full tutorial that breaks down every mechanic and what to do". Three layers: a guided first run, a lesson for every system, and a card the first time each system shows up in a real run.

## What the player sees

**Basic Training (about 6 minutes at 1×).** A fresh profile pressing Play gets "First Time? · Training · 6 min / Skip". Training is a 9-wave run on a fixed hand-drawn road (same shape on every screen), same seed for everyone, one idea at a time. A coach card points at the one thing to tap (spotlight dims the rest, pulsing amber ring, bobbing arrow), says one or two lines, and moves on only when the player does it. While the player must read or act the game is held; between steps a quiet line at the bottom says what is going on ("Wave 3: your towers fire on their own…", "Next wave in 9s. Spend credits…"). Every card has Skip Training (two taps).
Arc: core → core health bar (HP + shield) → road → build on the marked tile (tile, card, Build, each pointed at) → wave readout → Next → credits (first kill) → score → second tower → power (2/2, full) → **upgrading in depth**: built towers are tappable; the Upgrade button and its price; what it did (LV 1 → LV 2, Damage a → b, range, and the random bonus it rolled, read from the tower); why upgrade instead of building (power) → Threat Board chip → Juggernaut row (Plated) → build a Rail Lance → inspect a Juggernaut on the road → **second upgrade via the upgrade arrow** ("an arrow over a tower means you can afford its upgrade", the existing render.js cue) → Strike on a tight pack, aim, recharge bar → the swarm adapts (Arms Race: "93% of your damage is Kinetic, so it bred Kinetic Ward") → build the counter (Cryo, or Pulse if Cryo leads) → a Ward gene mark on the field → **LV 3 and a spec** (the player picks one), Ascend and Sell, briefly → bounty chip → the Most Wanted boss (Colossal Behemoth) → Bounty Claimed: Extract (jump doors shown, disabled: "Jump doors open in real runs") → Training Complete: +XP (exactly two levels on a fresh profile, `xpForLevel(0)+xpForLevel(1)` = 840 on the new curve), Level, talent points, then Main Menu / Talents / First Run.
Forgiving: core 150 + 20 shield; the core never drops below 1 ("CORE HELD · Training keeps your core alive. In a real run, this would end it", once); the boss circles back to the start of the road if it reaches the core (−25% core, toast) instead of ending the run. Enemies still leak and hurt.

**Field Manual** (the ? button; replaces How to Play; the Dictionary stays as its second tab). Basic Training on top, then 18 lessons with a ✓ when done and "N% complete" in the header. A lesson row opens a page: What it is / When it matters / What to do, and Play lesson (from inside a run: "Play lesson · saves your run"; the run is snapshotted and resumes from the hangar). Each lesson is a 1–2 wave scenario on the Training map with a preset board (or a walk through the hangar's own screens), ends on "Lesson complete" plus one line on what to do in a real run, and returns to its page. Lessons: Towers and elements · Upgrades, specs and selling · Power, reactor and core · Overcharge · Abilities and charges · The swarm adapts · Bounties and the purse (doors enabled here) · Crates and salvage · Veterans, affixes, resonance · Pylon and Forge · Storms and anomalies · Wormholes and checkpoints · Strain Assaults · Bosses, boons and Nemesis · Black Market and Cash In · Techniques · Ascend perks and Mastery · Daily Hunt (tour) · Talents and progression (tour). Basic Training is also on the New Run screen ("Training" in the footer).

**First-time cards in real runs** (existing `introCard`/`PROF.seenSys`, now with Learn more → the lesson page): storm, crate, vet, abil (existing) plus upg (can afford an upgrade, never upgraded: "Tap a tower to upgrade it…"), power (grid full), spare (2+ spare power from W6), wormhole, anomaly, boss (boss next wave), market, support (Pylon unlocked), tech (a Technique learned). Profiles with best wave 15+ get the new keys marked seen once (`seenSys.v58`), so Nic isn't flooded; Techniques stays (new to everyone).

## Decisions
- **Training runs never touch the profile.** `ghostRun()` now includes `G.tut`: no XP, kills, best wave, runs count, records, Hall of Fame, Daily, trophies/Nemesis, Swarm Collection, and no snapshot (closing the app mid-Training returns to the hangar; a real run's saved snapshot is untouched). The only write is the coach's reward and `PROF.tut`.
- **Same for everyone:** Training/lessons ignore talents, Laws and Supernova (`G.huntBuild` = the lesson's fixed build, empty for most), so `huntRun()` is true inside them (all towers buildable; the build menu shows only the lesson's `towers`).
- Old players: `Save.merge` sets `PROF.tut = { seen, done, old }` for any profile with runs or a best wave, so they are never prompted (they can still earn the reward once from the manual).
- After Basic Training: WELCOME, build-hint tiles, the evolution intro and the abilities card are marked as covered; zoom hint, storms, crates, veterans and the rest still introduce themselves.

## Adding a lesson (integrator)
One `LESSONS` entry (data.js) and one `PLANS[id]` list (ui.js Coach), same id. Steps: `{ id, when, skip, hold, at, t, b, ok, okFn, done, back, enter, leave, modal, tree }`; builders `info`, `call`, `build`, `tapTw`, `threatOpen`, `inThreat`, `intro`, `end`. `need()` hides a lesson until its code exists.
