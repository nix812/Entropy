## 1. Probe controls: command mode

Nic: "The probe controls are clunky. The fly out is intrusive when trying to use/move the probe."

**What was clunky (6.5.1, screenshots `test/out/ui652/probe_before_*`):**
- Any probe action opened the side panel: 270 px, 46% of the field at 667×375. Most of it is the upgrade list, which you
  do not need while steering.
- The panel opened on the side away from the probe. That is often the side you want to send it to.
- With the panel open, the Probe, Lift Off and Ready buttons hid. The Probe button could not toggle the mode off.
- A tap on a site opened a site card in the same panel. Sending needed a second button (Send probe) inside it.
- The only ways out were Done or ✕ in the panel. A tap on the probe did nothing.

**Now (`probe_after_*`):**
- Tap **Probe**, the probe, or a loot site. The probe is selected: the Probe button turns pale blue (pressed), and a small
  **bar** appears under it, in Ready's slot. Ready waits while the bar is up, as it does for a side panel. The side panel
  stays shut.
- The bar shows two lines. The first line says what the next tap does: "Tap the map to send it". The second line shows
  the probe's state: "Flying · 91% · 1.2K cargo" (★ for a jackpot). After that come **Recall**, **Upgrades** and **✕**.
  All bar controls are 44 px tall. Recall dims while the probe is docked or already returning.
- While selected, **every map tap sends the probe** (prGo, as before). A tap on a site sends it to work that site. A tap
  on the core recalls it. Pan and pinch work as usual.
- A tap on a site when nothing is selected shows that site in the bar: "Tap the site again to send", then "Wreckage · 2
  guards · 97 credits · 116 s". A second tap on the site sends the probe.
- **Upgrades** opens the full panel (the site in focus, hull, cargo, the upgrades, Recall, Done). Map taps still send the
  probe while it is open. Closing it (✕ or Done) returns to the bar.
- **Leave** with ✕, the Probe button again, a tap on the probe (unless the tap is beside the core, where it means recall),
  or Escape. The bar also closes on its own when the probe is lost, the run ends, Lift Off or relocation starts, a beacon
  is being placed, or another panel opens.
- A lost probe has no bar: the Probe button opens the panel with the rebuild, as before.
- While the bar is up, the corner feed shows its 3 newest lines (it can't reach the bar), and the zone banner sits low
  (64%).
- Lift Off and Probe are 44 px tall (were 40 and 34). Ready moves 10 px down: top 54 / 102 (were 52 / 92).
- Existing copy needs no change: the intro card ("Tap Probe, then tap a loot site or any spot") and the Dictionary still
  describe it correctly.

## 3. The Training checklist

Nic: "The tutorial list doesn't seem to update either as it progresses. Check for bugs there."

Reproduced with the new `test/tutcheck.py`, which plays Basic Training through the coach in three styles. Before
(screenshots `before_*_human_*`): at both sizes the strip read **"4. Read its bonus" from Wave 3 to the end**, while the
count rose 5/15 → 13/15. Root causes:
1. **"Read its bonus" ticked only through one button**: the Got it on the Affixes card (`mem.affix` in that step's
   `okFn`). A player who closes the tower panel there, or never opens it, never ticks it. The strip always shows the first
   unticked line, so it hung there for the rest of Training.
2. **Wrong order.** 6.2 (qolB) moved Strike and Veterans before the core upgrades, but the list kept the old order. Even
   a perfect run showed "9. Core: Shield" through the Strike and Veterans lessons, and lines 11–12 ticked out of order.
3. The flags for affix, dock and links lived in the coach's step memory (`mem`). It is cleared whenever the coach
   restarts, for example when a Training run resumes.

Fix (`ui.js`, Coach, `CHECK`):
- The list is in teaching order: …, Upgrade again, **Use Strike, Veteran**, Core: Shield, Core: Battery, Specialty,
  Bounty boss, Extract.
- "Read its bonus" ticks when any tower's Details tab shows an affix (the Got it still counts).
- Its flags live per run (`ck`, reset when `G` changes), not in `mem`.
- Each line names the step that teaches it. Once the coach is past that step, an unticked line is **missed**: it shows dim
  in the open list, the strip moves on to the next line, and doing it later still ticks it.

After (`after_*_human_*`): every line ticks on its own lesson step (within 2 coach steps) at both sizes. The strip
follows the lesson ("9/15 · 10. Veteran: tap Ready, pick" in Wave 5). In the skip style, the missed line is dim and the
strip moves past it.

**Docking rule (frV 6, the core's gun keeps firing when docked):** no step's done or skip test depends on the old rule.
`frDockT()` is unchanged, and the dock line ticks on any docking. The text that describes the old rule (yours to reword,
not changed here): the `docked` step ("The core fires the Rail Lance now: ×2 damage…"), the `lift` lesson intro ("on a
tower, it fires that tower's weapon"), and `frLandPanelHTML` ("A framed tower docks: the core fires its weapon at ×…").
`land` ("In the air the core's gun is off") and `corep` / `battery` still read correctly.
